FINIX Mule Account Detection (Graph Neural Network) Dataset Specification v2.0 (FINAL)
1. Objective
Develop a Graph Neural Network (GraphSAGE) to identify Mule Accounts by learning transactional relationships between bank accounts. The model outputs RecipientGNNScore (0–1), which is used by the Transaction Risk Score model.

6. Target Label
is_mule_account0 = Normal1 = MuleRecommended mule ratio: 1%–3%
7. Mule Behaviour Patterns
Collection Account – receives from many unrelated accounts and rapidly forwards funds.
Pass-through Account – incoming amount approximately equals outgoing amount.
Structuring – many similarly-sized transfers to multiple counterparties.
Dormant Account Activation – long inactivity followed by burst activity.
Circular Money Movement – A → B → C → A.
Layering – funds pass through multiple intermediary accounts.
